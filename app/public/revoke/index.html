Revoke a Corral agent

This page works with no Corral servers at all. It uses only your wallet and the public chain. You are the account owner; this sends one ordinary transaction from your wallet.

What revoke does: disables the agent's session on-chain. Every future attempt by the agent is rejected at the protocol level.
What revoke does not do: it cannot reverse a transaction that was already included in a block, and it does not move any funds — everything stays in your account.
Not connected.
